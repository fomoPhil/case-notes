# Thought Record

A CBT worksheet for noticing and re-examining automatic thoughts. Bring it to your next session.

## Situation

What happened? Where, when, and who was involved?

## Automatic thoughts

What went through your mind? Rate belief 0 to 100 percent.

## Emotions

What did you feel? Rate intensity 0 to 100 percent.

## Evidence for the thought

Facts that support the automatic thought.

## Evidence against the thought

Facts that do not support the automatic thought.

## Balanced thought

A fairer, more accurate way to see the situation.

## Outcome

Re-rate your emotion now. What will you do next?
