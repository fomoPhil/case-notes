Priya is a 26-year-old who presented in January 2026 with longstanding [[Emotional-Dysregulation]], intense and rapidly shifting emotions, and recurrent self-harm urges. Her difficulties are consistent with borderline personality traits (F60.3), and treatment follows a dialectical behavior therapy model built around a weekly diary card, chain analysis of target behaviors, and structured skills training across the four modules.

Early sessions established rapport, oriented her to DBT, and set up diary card tracking of self-harm urges, emotion intensity, and skills use. Her presentation is marked by strong [[Distress-Intolerance]], where painful emotions feel unbearable and demand immediate relief, and by [[Relational-Patterns]] in which fears of abandonment and perceived rejection trigger the sharpest escalations.

Her diary card now shows fewer target behaviors and higher skills use, with distress tolerance (TIPP, self-soothe, radical acceptance) and emotion regulation (opposite action, check the facts) generalizing to real-world triggers. She remains engaged, insightful, and increasingly able to ride out crises without acting on urges.
