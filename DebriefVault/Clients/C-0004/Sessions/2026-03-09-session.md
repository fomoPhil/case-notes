## Data
Priya arrived engaged and reported a relatively stable fortnight. Risk was screened: she denied suicidal ideation, intent, and plan, and denied any acute self-harm urge, noting baseline urges remain low on the diary card. She described a recurring wave of shame after perceived slights from her partner, part of her [[Relational-Patterns]].

## Assessment
We moved into [[emotion-regulation-skills]], starting with check the facts and opposite action. Using a shame episode from the week, we checked the facts and found her interpretation ("they are disgusted with me") outran the evidence. We then designed an opposite action, approaching rather than hiding, since the emotion did not fit the facts. She reflected, "So the move is to do the opposite of what shame is screaming at me." Naming that principle is solid progress toward Goal 3 and directly targets her [[Emotional-Dysregulation]]. She is beginning to treat emotions as data to be checked rather than commands to be obeyed.

## Plan
Continue [[diary-card]] with a check-the-facts note when a strong emotion appears. Homework: run one check the facts and, if warranted, an opposite action, then log it.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider mapping her emotional vulnerability factors (sleep, eating, routine).
- Possible focus on interpersonal effectiveness for the partner dynamic.
