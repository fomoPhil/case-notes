## Data
Intake session. Priya, 26, self-referred after a general practitioner suggested therapy for what she called "emotions that go from zero to a hundred." She described years of [[Emotional-Dysregulation]], intense mood shifts across a single day, and a history of self-harm urges that surface when distress feels unbearable, consistent with strong [[Distress-Intolerance]]. She reported cutting in the past, most recently several months ago. Risk was screened directly: she denied current suicidal ideation, intent, or plan, and denied any active urge to self-harm today, though she acknowledged urges "come and go." She named her sister and her studies as reasons for living.

## Assessment
Presentation is consistent with borderline personality traits (F60.3). Through a DBT lens, the core problem is pervasive emotion dysregulation with a biologically high sensitivity and slow return to baseline, paired with a limited repertoire of skillful responses. I oriented her to the DBT model, the four skills modules, and the role of target behaviors. Using the [[diary-card]], I set up daily tracking of self-harm urges (0 to 5), emotion intensity, and skills use. She engaged readily and said, "It actually helps to see it as a system and not just me being broken." That response signaled good buy-in to the frame.

## Plan
Establish diary card tracking as the foundation for Goal 1. Homework: complete the diary card each evening before the next session. Provide DBT orientation handout and crisis line number.
