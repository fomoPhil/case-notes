# Treatment Plan

## Goal 1: Reduce self-harm urges and eliminate self-harm behavior.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Track self-harm urges, emotion intensity, and skills use daily on the diary card.
  - Complete a chain analysis of any high-intensity urge to identify links and alternatives.
  - Build and use a written safety plan during crises.

## Goal 2: Build distress tolerance to survive crises without making them worse.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Learn and rehearse TIPP, self-soothe, and radical acceptance skills.
  - Identify personal crisis cues early and apply a skill before the urge peaks.

## Goal 3: Strengthen emotion regulation and reduce emotional vulnerability.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Practice opposite action and check the facts on recurring emotions.
  - Reduce baseline vulnerability through sleep, routine, and mastery activities.

## Goal 4: Improve interpersonal effectiveness in close relationships.
- Status: not-started
- Objectives:
  - Notice relational triggers linked to fears of rejection and abandonment.
  - Apply interpersonal effectiveness skills to ask for needs and set limits.
