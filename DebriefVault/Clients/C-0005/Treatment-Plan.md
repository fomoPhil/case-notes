# Treatment Plan

## Goal 1: Reprocess the target memory of the motor vehicle accident to full resolution.
- Status: achieved
- Objectives:
  - Establish a stable baseline for the target (SUDs and VOC) before desensitization.
  - Reduce SUDs on the accident memory to 0 to 1 and raise VOC on the positive cognition to 6 to 7.

## Goal 2: Reduce hypervigilance and avoidance while driving.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Identify and rate avoided driving situations, then re-engage them gradually as reprocessing gains generalize.
  - Track hypervigilance episodes and note reductions in scanning, gripping, and route avoidance.

## Goal 3: Strengthen self-regulation and stabilization resources.
- Status: achieved
- Objectives:
  - Install a calm place and at least one additional resource for use between sessions.
  - Confirm the client can self-soothe to a manageable arousal level before and after reprocessing.
