## Data
Intake and history taking session (EMDR Phase 1). Thomas, 38, described a serious motor vehicle accident several months ago in which another car struck his vehicle at an intersection. Since then he has experienced intrusive memories of the moment of impact, disrupted sleep, and strong hypervigilance while driving. He linked his distress to [[Trauma-Reprocessing]] needs, saying "the crash keeps replaying and I brace every time I get behind the wheel." His symptoms cluster around [[Anxiety]], including scanning other drivers and gripping the wheel. Risk was screened explicitly at intake and Thomas denied any current or historical thoughts of self-harm, suicide, or wanting to be dead.

## Assessment
Presentation is consistent with post-traumatic stress disorder (F43.10) from a single incident. I took a targeting history and identified the accident as the primary target memory. We began mapping the negative cognition, with Thomas endorsing "I am not safe," and a provisional positive cognition of "I am safe now." I explained the eight-phase EMDR model and how [[bilateral-stimulation]] would later be used to reprocess the target. Thomas responded, "it helps to know there is an actual plan," which suggested readiness and good rapport. This history taking supports early progress on Goal 1 (reprocess the target memory).

## Plan
Next session will focus on preparation and resourcing, including installing a calm place. No SUDs or VOC ratings were taken this session, as reprocessing has not yet begun. Provided psychoeducation on trauma responses and the window of tolerance.
