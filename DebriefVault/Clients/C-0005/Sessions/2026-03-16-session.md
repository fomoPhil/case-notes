## Data
Preparation and resourcing session (EMDR Phase 2), followed by assessment of the target (Phase 3). Thomas arrived engaged and reported he had practiced slow breathing during the week. We built a calm place, a quiet lakeside setting from his childhood, and completed resource installation with short sets of [[bilateral-stimulation]] to strengthen it. He described the resource vividly and said "I can actually feel my shoulders drop when I picture it," a clear in-session shift toward regulation.

## Assessment
With stabilization confirmed, I moved to assessment of the target memory of the accident. Thomas identified the moment of impact as the worst image, endorsed the negative cognition "I am not safe," and selected the positive cognition "I am safe now." We established a baseline: SUDs of 8 out of 10 and VOC of 2 out of 7 for the positive cognition. The disturbance located in his chest and hands. His steady tolerance of the calm place work, part of the [[Trauma-Reprocessing]] arc, supports Goal 3 (strengthen self-regulation resources) and readies him for desensitization. His arousal reflected ongoing [[Anxiety]] but stayed within his window of tolerance.

## Plan
