## Data
First desensitization session (EMDR Phase 4). Thomas reoriented to the calm place briefly, then returned to the target memory of the accident. He held the impact image, the negative cognition "I am not safe," and the body sensation in his chest as we began sets of [[bilateral-stimulation]]. He said "I keep forgetting that I actually got out and stood up."

## Assessment

By the end of the session SUDs had dropped to 6 and VOC had risen to 3, so the target moved from SUDs 8 to 6 and VOC 2 to 3. The shift toward remembering his own agency softened the "powerless" edge of his [[Anxiety]] and began to loosen his [[Avoidance-and-Approach]] pattern around driving. This progress supports Goal 1 (reprocess the target memory).
