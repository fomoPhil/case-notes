# Treatment Plan

## Goal 1: Reduce catastrophic misinterpretation of benign bodily sensations.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Complete a thought record after each significant health worry, capturing the trigger sensation and the catastrophic prediction.
  - Generate at least one balanced alternative interpretation per record and rate belief before and after.

## Goal 2: Reduce safety behaviors that maintain the health anxiety cycle.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Track daily frequency of body scanning, online symptom checking, and reassurance seeking.
  - Apply graded response prevention, starting with delaying and then dropping one checking behavior.

## Goal 3: Build tolerance of uncertainty about health through behavioral experiments.
- Status: not-started
- Objectives:
  - Design and run a first behavioral experiment testing a catastrophic prediction against what actually happens.
  - Review outcomes to update beliefs about the danger of uncertainty.
