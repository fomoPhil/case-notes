## Data

Jane reported ongoing difficulties in her relationship with her adult daughter, noting that communication has been strained since her husband left. Jane disclosed using excessive ice cream consumption as a coping mechanism for these interpersonal stressors. Therapist introduced breathing techniques to address this.

## Assessment

Jane is currently facing barriers to her treatment goals due to significant interpersonal conflict and emotional distress regarding her family dynamics. Her reliance on ice cream suggests a need for increased willingness to experience difficult emotions rather than utilizing avoidant behaviors. She is making progress in identifying these patterns, but the current stressor requires focused intervention on emotion regulation.

Therapist provided breathing techniques as homework to address emotional distress. Jane's response was receptive to the new tool. The next clinical step is to monitor her ability to utilize these techniques instead of avoidant coping behaviors during family conflicts.
