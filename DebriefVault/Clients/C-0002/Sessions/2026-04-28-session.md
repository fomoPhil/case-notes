## Data
Jane arrived noticeably lighter in affect. She set a 9:30 pm work cutoff and reported holding it five of seven nights, protecting a consistent screen-free wind-down of reading and stretching. Her sleep log showed onset now averaging around 35 minutes, with two nights under 20. She still had one difficult night before a family event, but said she "used the tools instead of fighting the bed." Daytime [[Anxiety]] persists in bursts but feels "less sticky."

## Assessment
This session centered committed action as the bridge between values and behavior. Using [[willingness-and-acceptance]], we reviewed the family-event night and framed her wakefulness as an experience she allowed rather than a failure, which preserved her willingness stance under stress. I paired this with [[values-clarification]] to reconnect the wind-down routine to being present and rested for the people and work she cares about, reinforcing the behavior with intrinsic meaning rather than sleep metrics alone. She responded, "The routine stopped feeling like a rule and started feeling like a choice." That is clear progress on the sleep-routine goal, with the consistency barrier now largely addressed through the fixed cutoff. [[Sleep]] onset is trending down steadily.

## Plan
Homework: maintain the 9:30 pm cutoff and screen-free window, continue willingness practice as needed, and note which values the routine serves each night. Begin thinning the formal sleep log toward self-monitoring.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider a relapse-prevention frame for high-stress weeks.
- Possible focus on generalizing willingness to daytime anxiety bursts.
