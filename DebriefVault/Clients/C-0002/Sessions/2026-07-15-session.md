## Data
After a longer gap due to travel, Jane reported durable gains. Sleep onset now sits around 20 minutes on most nights, and she described bedtime as "mostly boring now, in a good way." The screen-free wind-down has become habitual and she maintains it without logging. Daytime [[Anxiety]] still appears before deadlines, but she said it "shows up, says its piece, and moves on." She raised one recent stretch of poor sleep during a work crunch and wanted to review how she handled it.

## Assessment
Jane demonstrates a consolidated willingness stance and a workable relationship with worry. Reviewing the work-crunch week, we used [[willingness-and-acceptance]] to normalize the temporary uptick as context-driven rather than a return of the problem, and she reapplied [[cognitive-defusion]] on her own to unhook from catastrophic "I will never sleep again" thoughts. She reported the flare resolved within three nights without abandoning her routine, telling me, "I trusted it would settle, and it did." This self-directed recovery is strong evidence of progress on both treatment-plan goals: willingness to experience anxiety without avoidance, and a stable, restorative [[Sleep]] routine. No active barriers were identified this session.

Move toward a maintenance and relapse-prevention phase. Homework: keep the wind-down routine, deploy defusion and willingness as needed, and draft an early-warning plan for high-stress periods. Discussed spacing future sessions.
