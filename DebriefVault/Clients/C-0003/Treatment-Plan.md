# Treatment Plan

## Goal 1: Reduce Deborah's triangulation between Marcus and Ray so co-parenting decisions move directly between the adults responsible.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Route each co-parenting request about Jamie directly to Ray rather than relaying it through Jamie or Marcus.
  - Notice and name in the moment when she is being pulled into the middle of a two-party conflict, and step out of the relay role.

## Goal 2: Establish clear, consistent subsystem boundaries in the blended household.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Differentiate from the enmeshed closeness with Chloe by holding at least one bounded, age-appropriate limit per week without collapsing into guilt.
  - Rebuild parental authority with Ethan by agreeing shared house rules with Marcus and presenting them as a united couple subsystem.

## Goal 3: Strengthen blended-family integration and steady co-parenting communication.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Hold a brief structured family check-in so household issues are raised openly rather than through side alliances.
  - Maintain a low-conflict, businesslike co-parenting channel with Ray, using written logistics for handovers and schedules.
