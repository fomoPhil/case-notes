## Data
Deborah reported a modest win: she and Marcus agreed two rules (devices off at dinner, weeknight bedtimes) and told all three children together. Ethan tested it twice but complied the third night. She said, "when Marcus stood next to me, I actually felt like I had a leg to stand on." The relay with Ray, however, continued, and she passed a scheduling message she disagreed with.

## Assessment
This was the first real couple-subsystem enactment and it held, which is early progress on the boundary goal. I deepened [[detriangulation]] work by focusing on the Ray channel. We practiced her stepping out of the middle: instead of relaying Ray's message to Marcus and back, she would answer Ray directly about Jamie's logistics. Deborah rehearsed a short, businesslike reply aloud and noticed her heart racing, connecting it to the family-of-origin fear that direct contact means conflict. Naming that link to old [[Relational-Patterns]] was the key moment. The [[Family-Conflict]] around Ethan eased slightly when authority was shared, confirming the structural hypothesis, while the Ray triangle remains the harder edge.

## Plan
Assigned one experiment: reply to Ray's next logistics message directly, keeping it factual, without routing it through Marcus. Continue the two agreed house rules as a couple.
