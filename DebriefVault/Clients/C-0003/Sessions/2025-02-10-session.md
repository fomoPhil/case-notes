## Data
Deborah arrived with notes she had made on the week's flashpoints. The clearest pattern was handover day: Jamie returns from Ray's, is irritable, and the whole household tightens. She reported that Marcus and Ray have never spoken directly and that "everything goes through me or through Jamie." She was curious rather than defensive today and wanted to keep building the map.

## Assessment
We extended [[genogram-mapping]] back to Deborah's parents and grandparents, marking relationships as close, conflicted, or cut off. A striking multigenerational [[Relational-Patterns]] shape emerged: her mother had sat between two feuding relatives for years, and the family's habitual response to tension was to stop speaking altogether. Deborah went quiet, then said, "I am literally doing what my mum did." Naming that connection was the pivotal moment of the session. The genogram also made the present-day [[Family-Conflict]] legible as a structure rather than a series of unrelated arguments, which advances the v1 goal of understanding the family map. I used the visual to normalize her position without excusing its cost.

## Plan
Asked Deborah to add anyone I had missed to the genogram and to note when she catches herself relaying messages between two people who could speak directly. Flagged that next session we would look at where she sits in the current map.
