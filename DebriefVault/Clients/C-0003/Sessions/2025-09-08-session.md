## Data
Deborah returned after the summer describing a significant setback. A dispute over holiday dates for Jamie collapsed the direct channel: Ray was inflexible, Deborah felt cornered, and she pulled Marcus in to "back her up," which reignited the old triangle. There was a week of near-silence at home. She said, "I fell straight back into the switchboard the moment it got hard."

## Assessment
I normalized the relapse as expected under stress rather than a failure, which visibly eased her self-criticism. We used [[detriangulation]] to trace exactly where the triangle re-formed: the point of overwhelm was when Ray refused to negotiate, and recruiting Marcus felt safer than staying in direct contact. This maps precisely onto the family-of-origin [[Relational-Patterns]] of retreating from one-to-one conflict. Deborah recognized it herself: "I only reach for a third person when I am scared of the two-person conversation." That was the session's turning point. The [[Family-Conflict]] spike confirms the pattern's tenacity, and the barrier is clear: she lacks a fallback for high-heat moments when direct contact stalls, so progress on the detriangulation goal stalled this cycle.

## Plan
Built a simple protocol: when a direct exchange with Ray overheats, pause and resume in writing rather than escalating through Marcus. Asked her to keep Marcus as a co-parent to Chloe and Ethan, not as a shield in the Ray relationship.
