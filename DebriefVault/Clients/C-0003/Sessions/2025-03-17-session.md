## Data
Deborah described a difficult fortnight. A dispute over Jamie's football schedule turned into three separate one-to-one conversations, all routed through her, ending with Marcus and Ray each annoyed at the other and neither having spoken. She said she spent two days "carrying messages I did not agree with."

## Assessment
Completing the [[genogram-mapping]] made the current triangle unmistakable: Deborah sits at the apex between Marcus and Ray, with Jamie's needs as the recurring content. I named this as triangulation, the classic pattern where two people manage their tension through a third, and linked it directly to the family-of-origin [[Relational-Patterns]] we had mapped. Deborah responded, "so I inherited the seat in the middle," and looked both unsettled and relieved. This recognition is real progress toward the v1 goal of understanding the family map, though the [[Family-Conflict]] itself remains high. I was careful to frame the pattern as maintained by everyone's habits, not caused by her, to reduce the shame that had kept her over-functioning in the relay role.

## Plan
Assigned an observation task: for the next two weeks, notice each moment she is handed a message to pass along, and simply note it without changing anything yet. This builds the awareness that detriangulation will later require.
