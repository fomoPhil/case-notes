## Assessment
This session consolidated differentiation across every relationship strand. The Chloe example is the clearest evidence yet that warmth and separateness can coexist, retiring Deborah's long-held fear that a boundary equals rejection. I framed her restraint as mature [[detriangulation]] applied within the parent-child dyad: she stayed connected without absorbing or managing Chloe's emotional world. The quick, contained resolution with Ray shows the co-parenting channel is now robust. The family-of-origin [[Relational-Patterns]] that once drove relay and cutoff no longer dominate her responses, and the [[Family-Conflict]] that brought her to therapy is largely reorganized into ordinary, workable friction. We began discussing what maintenance and eventual spacing of sessions might look like.
