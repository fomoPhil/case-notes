## Data
The holidays intensified logistics: who has the children when, competing family gatherings, and Ray requesting a schedule change late. Deborah reported holding the written channel with Ray "mostly," slipping once into relaying through Jamie. With Chloe she managed one bounded response and found Chloe "was actually fine, she just wanted me to listen."

## Assessment
Mixed but encouraging. The Chloe experiment produced a small, important disconfirmation of Deborah's fear that separateness equals abandonment, and she named it: "she did not fall apart, and neither did I." That was the session's high point and marks genuine movement on the boundary goal. The single slip with Jamie showed how holiday stress still pulls her toward the old triangle, which I framed as information rather than failure. We used [[detriangulation]] to plan for the high-load season, distinguishing logistics (route to Ray directly, in writing) from the children's feelings (hold, do not relay). The [[Family-Conflict]] remained contained overall, and the family-of-origin [[Relational-Patterns]] exerted less pull than in previous high-stress periods.

## Plan
Assigned a written master schedule shared with Ray to reduce late-notice negotiation. Continue bounded, warm responses with Chloe. Agreed to review the plan formally in the new year.
