## Data
Deborah reported recovering the direct channel with Ray using the written-pause protocol. When a handover disagreement flared, she wrote, "let me come back to you tomorrow," and did, calmly. Ray responded in kind. She said, "for the first time I ended a Ray conversation without Marcus even knowing it happened."

## Assessment
This was clear recovery from the summer setback and solid progress toward the [[detriangulation]] goal. We reviewed the episode step by step, reinforcing that keeping the two-person conversation two-person is the whole move. Deborah noticed her anxiety at direct contact had dropped from what she rated as "an eight to about a four." I linked the gain to differentiation: she held her own position without either capitulating or recruiting a third party, a direct departure from the family-of-origin [[Relational-Patterns]] of cutoff. The wider [[Family-Conflict]] at home was noticeably lower this cycle because Marcus was freed from being pulled into the Ray dispute. The remaining growth edge is the enmeshment with Chloe, which we agreed to turn to next.

## Plan
Assigned Deborah to keep using the written-pause tool and to log her anxiety rating each time she contacts Ray directly. Previewed a focus on the over-close pattern with Chloe.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider mapping the enmeshment cycle with Chloe in detail.
- Possible focus on tolerating Chloe's disappointment as a sign of healthy separateness.
