## Data
Focus shifted to Chloe. Deborah described a typical evening: Chloe texts her about a friendship dispute, Deborah drops her own tasks, gets anxious alongside her, and stays up late problem-solving on Chloe's behalf. She admitted she sometimes uses the closeness with Chloe as proof she is "a good stepmother," especially when Ethan rebuffs her.

## Assessment
We mapped the enmeshment as a boundary-thin loop where Deborah's emotional state fuses with Chloe's. I extended [[detriangulation]] thinking to this dyad: Deborah has partly triangulated Chloe into her own need for validation, which keeps both of them from separateness. When I asked what would happen if she offered warmth without absorbing the distress, Deborah said, "I would have to trust that Chloe can cope, and honestly I do not, yet." That candor was the key moment. This reframes the [[Family-Conflict]] as partly maintained by over-involvement, not just under-authority, and deepens the picture of her [[Relational-Patterns]]. Progress on the boundary goal is real but partial: she can now see the loop, though stepping out of it still feels like abandonment to her.

## Plan
Assigned one bounded response to Chloe this week: listen and empathize, but hand the problem back with confidence rather than solving it. Asked her to notice the guilt without acting on it.

- Consider how holiday season pressures may test every boundary at once.
- Possible focus on differentiating warmth from rescue.
