## Data
Deborah described a flare with Ethan: she asked him to come off his console for dinner, he ignored her, and when Marcus was out she "just gave up." She said the incident left her feeling like "a guest in my own kitchen." She also reported that Chloe had been upset that week and Deborah had cancelled her own plans to sit with her.

## Assessment
I introduced the idea of differentiation, holding a clear self while staying connected, and framed both the Ethan and Chloe episodes as the same underlying difficulty read from opposite ends. I began [[detriangulation]] conceptually: authority with Ethan cannot be rebuilt while decisions detour around Deborah and Marcus rather than through them. When I named that she and Marcus were not operating as a couple subsystem, she said, "we never actually agreed the rules, so of course Ethan ignores me." That was the working insight of the session. This surfaces the [[Family-Conflict]] as structural and clarifies the entrenched [[Relational-Patterns]], but the barrier is clear: no shared parental stance yet exists, which blocks progress on boundary goals until Marcus is involved.

## Plan
Assigned Deborah and Marcus to agree just two non-negotiable house rules together this week and present them jointly. Asked her to notice the guilt that arises when she does not drop everything for Chloe.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider a session that includes Marcus to build the couple subsystem.
- Possible focus on tolerating Chloe's distress without collapsing the boundary.

Clinical judgment and final session planning remain the therapist's responsibility.
