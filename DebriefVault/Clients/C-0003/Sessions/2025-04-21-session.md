## Data
Deborah brought her observation notes. She had logged eleven relay moments in two weeks and was surprised how many involved the stepchildren, not just Jamie. She spontaneously described two very different relationships: with Chloe (15) she is "joined at the hip," texting all day, and with Ethan (13) she feels "invisible, like I have no standing to ask him anything."

## Assessment
Using the completed genogram from [[genogram-mapping]], I mapped the two contrasting subsystems inside the home. With Chloe there is enmeshment: boundaries are thin, and Deborah's mood tracks Chloe's minute to minute. With Ethan there is an eroded-authority standoff, where Deborah has stepped back so far that no parental subsystem holds him. She said, "I overdo it with one and disappear with the other," which captured the split precisely and was the key insight of the session. These distinct [[Relational-Patterns]] explain why blanket rules keep failing and why the [[Family-Conflict]] feels so uneven. This refines our understanding and sets up the next phase of work.

## Plan
Asked Deborah to notice one moment of over-closeness with Chloe and one moment of stepping back from Ethan each week, without yet changing them. Previewed that the plan would soon shift toward boundaries and stepping out of the middle.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider introducing the concept of differentiation.
- Possible focus on what a bounded but warm stance with each child could look like.
