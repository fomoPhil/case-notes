Deborah is a 44-year-old woman who entered therapy navigating the strain of a newly blended household. She remarried her husband Marcus in 2023, bringing together her son Jamie (from her relationship with her ex-partner Ray) and Marcus's two children, Chloe and Ethan. She presented with escalating [[Family-Conflict]] over household rules, discipline, and loyalty, alongside significant co-parenting stress with Ray. Treatment uses a family systems framework, treating symptoms as expressions of the whole household's structure rather than any one person's fault.

Early work centered on [[genogram-mapping]], which surfaced multigenerational [[Relational-Patterns]]: her own mother had been triangulated between warring relatives, and emotional cutoff was the family's default response to conflict. The map made visible that Deborah now occupies the same middle position, triangulated between Marcus and Ray around the children. It also clarified two distinct strains inside the home: an enmeshed, boundary-thin closeness with her stepdaughter Chloe, and an eroded-authority standoff with her stepson Ethan.

The middle and later phases focused on [[detriangulation]], boundary-setting, and differentiation. Deborah practiced routing co-parenting decisions directly to the person concerned rather than relaying them through the children, and worked to hold a warm but bounded stance with both stepchildren. Progress was uneven, with a summer setback around holiday logistics, but by the most recent sessions she reported steadier co-parenting communication with Ray, clearer household expectations, and a calmer sense of self when family pressure spiked. Risk has consistently been not indicated; it was screened and denied at intake and has not been a clinical concern since.
