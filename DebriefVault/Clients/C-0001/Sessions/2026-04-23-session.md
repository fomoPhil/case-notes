## Data
Bob reported a steadier fortnight. He has widened his scheduled activities to include a weekly five-a-side football game and reported looking forward to it during the working week, "which used to feel impossible." A stressful moment arose when a deadline slipped due to another team's delay, and he initially blamed himself entirely. He used his coping card and a thought record to separate his contribution from factors outside his control. Risk was screened and denied.

## Assessment
Continued [[behavioral-activation]] has produced anticipatory pleasure, a marker that reward sensitivity is recovering, which supports Goal 2. On the deadline episode I used [[socratic-questioning]] to apportion responsibility realistically, addressing the [[Work-Undermining]] tendency to absorb blame for systemic issues. Bob concluded, "I did my part on time, the delay was not mine to own," and visibly relaxed as he said it. That reattribution is a specific in-session response and reflects the [[Worthlessness]] belief losing its grip. Belief conviction was 65 percent. The barrier now is generalizing these gains to higher-stakes situations, such as direct evaluation by his manager.

## Plan
Continue activity scheduling and thought records. Begin drafting a graded hierarchy of workplace feedback situations in preparation for exposure work.

## Next Session Considerations
- Consider ranking feedback situations from least to most threatening.
- Possible focus on the upcoming performance review as a top-of-hierarchy item.
