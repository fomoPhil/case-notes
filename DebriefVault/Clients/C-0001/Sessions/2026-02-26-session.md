## Data
Bob reported that the prediction-accuracy column had been "eye-opening." Across five recorded stressors, none of his catastrophic predictions came true, and he noted this in his own handwriting. He submitted a report without over-checking and received neutral-to-positive feedback. He still described a background hum of dread before meetings. Risk was screened and Bob denied any thoughts of self-harm or wishing he were not here. He said his mood was "the steadiest it has been in months."

## Assessment
The accumulating evidence base is beginning to soften the [[Worthlessness]] belief; conviction for "I am worthless" dropped to 80 percent, the first meaningful shift. I continued [[cognitive-restructuring]], this time targeting the double standard between how he judges himself and others, a hallmark of his [[Perfectionism]]. When I asked him to state the rule out loud, he said, "Everyone else gets to be human except me," and laughed at how stark it sounded. That recognition is a concrete in-session change and supports Goal 1. He remains reliant on external reassurance, which is the current barrier.

## Plan
Assigned [[thought-records]] focused specifically on the double-standard distortion. Introduced the idea of behavioral experiments and previewed scheduling non-work activities to test worth independent of performance.

- Possible focus on reducing reassurance-seeking behaviors.
