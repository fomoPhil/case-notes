## Data
Bob presented lower this session. A restructuring rumor at work had reignited fears of redundancy, and he described a return of the sense that his value hinges entirely on his job. In this context he disclosed passive thoughts, describing moments over the past week of "not really wanting to be here" and a sense that "my family might have an easier time without me." He was clear these were passive and unwelcome, denied any active wish to die, and denied any plan or intent. See Risk section. He had still completed his worksheets and attended football.

## Assessment
The workplace threat has temporarily reactivated the [[Worthlessness]] core belief, and belief conviction rose slightly to 62 percent from 58, consistent with a stressor-linked fluctuation rather than a reversal of overall progress. The passive ideation is understood as an expression of the [[Work-Undermining]] equation of job loss with personal erasure. I used [[cognitive-restructuring]] to separate the rumor (uncertain, external) from his worth (not contingent on employment), and reinforced [[behavioral-activation]] to protect routine and connection. Bob responded, "Written down like that, the leap from a rumor to my family being better off is huge," which restored some perspective in session. Progress on Goal 2 is the current protective lever.

## Plan
Reviewed reasons for living and discussed a safety plan (see Risk). Agreed to monitor mood daily on a simple scale and to contact the practice or crisis line if passive thoughts intensify or any intent emerges. Continue activities and worksheets.

## Risk
- Assessed: Yes
- Ideation: Passive thoughts of "not wanting to be here" and that "others would be better off," occurring in brief moments over the past week, experienced as unwelcome.
- Plan, intent, means: No active wish to die. No plan. No intent. No access to or consideration of means reported.
- Protective factors: Strong attachment to young family, consistent treatment engagement, clear future orientation, football and social connections intact, help-seeking attitude.
- Interventions taken: Reviewed reasons for living, discussed and agreed a safety plan (warning signs, coping steps, support contacts, crisis line), agreed daily mood monitoring and to re-assess next session.
