## Data
Bob described a week where he received written feedback on a proposal containing both praise and two suggested changes. His old pattern would have been to fixate only on the criticism. This time he reported reading the whole message and thinking, "two changes out of a solid proposal is not a failure." He still felt a flush of shame but it passed within minutes rather than lasting the evening. Risk was screened and he denied any thoughts of self-harm or hopelessness. He completed all worksheets.

## Assessment
The proposal episode shows the [[Perfectionism]] filter loosening: he processed mixed feedback proportionally rather than collapsing it into total failure. I used [[socratic-questioning]] to examine what "solid but improvable" says about his competence, and [[cognitive-restructuring]] to build a balanced summary statement he could reuse. His self-generated reframe about "two changes out of a solid proposal" is a concrete response demonstrating internalized skills, marking strong progress on Goal 1. Belief conviction for "I am worthless" was 68 percent. The residual shame flush indicates the [[Worthlessness]] belief is quieter but not yet extinguished.

## Plan
Assigned a brief coping card summarizing his balanced statement for use before opening feedback. Continue thought records and value-based activities.
