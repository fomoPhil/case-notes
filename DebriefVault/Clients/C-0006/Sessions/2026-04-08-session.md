## Data
Intake session. Eleanor, age 52 and self-referred, described roughly twenty years of a low, grinding depressive mood that she called "just how I am," worsened since her mother died three months ago. She reported longstanding [[Worthlessness]] and a recurring difficulty in close relationships, saying she tends to "keep people at arm's length before they can tell me I'm not enough." Her history included a mother she experienced as exacting and rarely satisfied. Risk was screened directly: she denied any suicidal or self-harm ideation, intent, or plan, and named her sister and her work as reasons for living. Affect was flat but she was articulate and engaged.

## Assessment
Presentation is consistent with persistent depressive disorder (F34.1) with recent bereavement reactivating older material. Through a psychodynamic lens, her chronic self-criticism and her guardedness in relationships appear to share an early root in feeling unseen. This session prioritized building the therapeutic alliance so the unconscious relational template could later be observed. I attended lightly to how she related to me, an early groundwork for [[transference-interpretation]], noting warmth alongside a watchful quality. When I reflected that she seemed to be checking whether I would find her wanting, she paused and said, "That is exactly what I do with everyone." That specific recognition suggested good capacity for insight and marked initial progress toward understanding her [[Relational-Patterns]].

## Plan
Establish the alliance and begin mapping her relational history and her [[Grief-and-Loss]]. Homework: notice moments this week when she braces for criticism. Psychoeducation on psychodynamic work provided.
