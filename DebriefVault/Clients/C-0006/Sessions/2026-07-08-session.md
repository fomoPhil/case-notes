## Data
Eleanor arrived warmer and more settled. She described a phone call with her sister in which she had "caught myself deciding she was criticizing me, and just asked her instead of pulling away." The conversation had gone well and left her surprised. She spoke about her mother with more sorrow and less bitterness, and referred back easily to earlier sessions.

## Assessment
The working-through is consolidating. Eleanor is now able to observe her template as it activates rather than being fully inside it, which is loosening the grip of her [[Relational-Patterns]]. In the room the transference was lighter; when a flicker of the old expectation appeared, I offered a brief [[transference-interpretation]] and she met it herself, saying "I know, I started waiting for you to be disappointed, and then I remembered that is the old script." That specific response shows the insight has moved from intellectual to lived, and the alliance is visibly stronger. Her [[Grief-and-Loss]] is maturing into a truer mourning that can hold both love and disappointment, which is gradually softening her [[Worthlessness]]. The tentative shift with her sister is early behavioral evidence of change and marks meaningful progress across Goals 1 and 2. Intellectualization still surfaces under stress, so continued attention to affect tolerance remains warranted.

## Plan
