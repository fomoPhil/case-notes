## Data
Eleanor returned having thought about the previous session all fortnight. She opened by saying she had noticed herself "doing the same thing with my sister, deciding she was annoyed with me before she said a word." She was more reflective than defended and stayed with feeling for longer stretches, though she still reached for analysis when the material grew tender.

## Assessment
This session the interpretation was offered fully and worked with. I named the pattern directly, using [[transference-interpretation]] to connect her live experience of me as critical to her lifelong experience of her mother, and then to the way she now casts nearly everyone, including her sister, in that same withholding role. Eleanor took it in rather than deflecting. "So I keep hiring people to play her," she said, and then, more quietly, "and then I make sure they let me down." That specific response reflects genuine insight into her relational template and its origins in her [[Worthlessness]] and unresolved [[Grief-and-Loss]]. We touched the repetition at the heart of her [[Relational-Patterns]]: the way she unconsciously recreates the disappointing relationship in order to master it, and how that keeps her isolated. This is clear progress toward Goal 1, with the working-through only beginning.

## Plan
Continue working through the interpretation; watch for the pattern in the transference and in outside relationships. Homework: when she notices herself casting someone as her mother, name it internally and stay curious.

- Possible focus on grieving the mother she needed and did not have.
