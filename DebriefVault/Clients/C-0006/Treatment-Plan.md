# Treatment Plan

## Goal 1: Understand and revise the relational template that drives recurring difficulty in close relationships.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Notice and name the pattern of anticipating criticism and withdrawing to protect against it.
  - Use the therapeutic relationship, including transference as it arises, to observe the template in real time.

## Goal 2: Work through the grief for her mother and its links to longstanding low self-worth.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Allow mourning to include disappointment and unmet longing, not only loss.
  - Trace how self-worth became contingent on approval that was rarely given.

## Goal 3: Build tolerance for painful affect without relying on intellectualization and other defenses.
- Status: in-progress
- Objectives:
  - Recognize defenses as they operate in session.
  - Stay with feeling long enough to gain insight rather than explaining it away.
