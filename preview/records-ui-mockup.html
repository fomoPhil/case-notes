Why this design: the vault is presented as a client record system, not a file browser. Folders and .md files never appear; the app owns filenames and paths so renames can't break the agent's links. Every document renders as a finished page (Newsreader on paper) with three universal actions: Edit, Download PDF, Email draft. Email is always a Mail draft, matching the app's never-auto-send rule. The Library is where agent-created worksheets live so they can be reused across clients instead of being one-offs. Hover a document card on screen 1 to see the rename / download / email quick actions.
